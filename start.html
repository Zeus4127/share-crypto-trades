PREVIEW

Start here

Share/Crypto Trades is a share and crypto scanner. Every evening it looks over the ASX and the crypto market, picks out a few things worth a look, and keeps an honest, dated score of how its past picks went. Each tab is below in a line or two: tap a name to open it.

The short version: start with Tonight, then Status. Nothing on the site has yet proven it beats simply holding the ASX 200 fund, so treat everything as something to look at, not an instruction to buy.

Status

The home page. Every idea the system is testing, ranked by its chance of making money, with its win rate so far.

Most tests use pretend money. A test only counts once it has 30 or more results.

Tonight

The one-minute answer for the next trading day: is there anything worth buying at the 10am open, and why.

If it says the picks are old, the evening run has not made new ones yet.

Shares

Look up any ASX share by code or name: its chart, recent moves and what the scanner thinks.

Tap any share anywhere on the site to open it here.

Research

The scanner's wider view: shares climbing steadily, industries, the economy, news and the calendar.

Background reading, not a list of buys.

Spikes

Shares that jumped today, biggest first, and whether jumps like that kept going in the past.

Watch only: jumps like these did not reliably keep going.

Ready to buy

How close the shares and crypto sides are to trading real money, as a score out of 100.

Nothing has passed every check yet, so nothing here is a real-money buy.

Research feed

What videos, podcasts, papers and the news are saying about our shares, coins and ways of trading.

Ideas worth testing, never buys.

Who's winning

People whose trades are public: company directors buying their own shares, big investors, and crypto leaderboards, with how often their buys won.

Copying them did not beat the market in past years; a pretend-money copy is checking whether it works now.

Risk vs reward

The steadiest mix found so far: half gold, half a short list of growing mid-sized companies.

Steadier is not the same as bigger returns.

Crypto market

The whole crypto market at a glance: the biggest coins, today's winners and losers, and sudden jumps in trading.

Crypto can move 10% or more in a day.

Crypto chart

Live crypto price charts, like an exchange screen, with practice buy and sell buttons.

The buttons are practice only: no real money moves.

Token watch

Brand-new crypto tokens, checked for common scams and scored.

Most new tokens fail. Look, never buy on this alone.

Crypto safe

Crypto ideas that dropped less than about 10% at their worst, with this week's picks.

Pretend money, scored a week later.

Crypto risky

Crypto ideas that can drop 20% or more, with this week's picks.

Pretend money. Bigger swings both ways.

Records

Every test's record, kept and dated before its results came in, so nothing is changed after the fact.

Where to check that the scores are honest.
