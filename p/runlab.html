What 10 years of the ASX says

A clean chart alone is not the signalBuying smooth 3-month climbers and selling after a month or at -10% averaged -0.4% a trade over 2016-2026 (1,767 trades), behind STW. Only 3-5% reached +20% inside a month. zac's CLIMB-1/2/3 (6 Nov) found the same after 2020: 0 of 110 versions passed.
Why runs startOf 1,842 runs of +50% within 3 months: trading 2x the usual dollar volume doubled the chance of a run, and a new 52-week high on 2x volume made it 2.6x as likely. But those signs raise the chance of a 30% fall about as much. Smooth shares ran less often, and fell less often.
You do not have to be firstOnce a real run was visible (+15% from its low), a median +53% was still left to the top.
Why they endAt the top, a typical run sat 27% above its 50-day average and had risen 16% in its last 10 days: speeding up is the warning. After the top it fell a median 31% within 3 months.
The exit that kept the mostSelling after a 15% fall from the top kept about 30% of each move. Selling on the first close under the 20- or 50-day average kept almost none: it shakes you out early.
Best rule found, not provenNew high on 2x volume, not already jumpy, market rising, sold on a 15% trail: 615 trades, +1.4% a trade (+1.9% vs STW), 7 of 10 years positive, but 39% winners and two-thirds of the profit from the top 10% of trades. Luck score 2.4, found after trying many versions.
Those figures come from a cloud study on 11 Nov 2026 that read prices up to Oct 2026, including the sealed Nov 2025 - Apr 2026 window, so they are not clean. That is why every way of picking above is now being judged only on sealed forward lists from Fri 13 Nov 2026.
