Shrunk = the live figure pulled toward what that kind of strategy did in 2016–22 (its family, then all strategies), by how much the live data can be trusted. On history, after one week the shrunk figure predicted the next 26 weeks with 94% less squared error than the raw win % (and 95% for the mean). Detectable edge today = the smallest true edge over STW the record could already tell from luck.
