How to read it. Every time the system says "x% chance", it is graded like a weather forecast. Brier: the average squared miss (0 is perfect; lower is better). BSS: how much better than always saying the average rate (above 0 = some skill; the interval must clear 0). RMS gap: the typical gap between what was said and what happened, per tenth of the forecasts (0.03 = 3 points). AUC: does a higher number pick the winners (0.5 = a coin; 1 = perfect). Live results cover only a few days so far, and days move together, so read the intervals, not the point.
