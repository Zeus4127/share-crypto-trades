What the crypto system has found so far

Most crypto ideas have failed so farThe crypto system has tried 150+ rules since Oct 2026 (CRYPTO-1 to 16), each against holding Bitcoin after fees. Chasing winners, momentum, hourly patterns and altcoin funding all failed; nothing has beaten holding BTC.
The one pass is not a price betBitcoin carry (hold BTC and short the same amount of its perp, collecting the funding fee) passed the luck bar, but made only about +1.5% in its held-back year. It is on paper as CW-CARRY-1.
New listings are the worstBuying newly listed coins was the worst rule seen: a median -13% in the first week.
Why test runs anywayRyan's question is different: not 'hold the winners' but 'get on a clean climb, ride it, and get out on a trail'. That exact shape has not been tested on crypto, so it is tested here from scratch, forward only.
Crypto swings about twice as much as an ASX share, so every limit here is wider (daily wobble, falls, trails). These ways are untested: they are judged only on sealed forward lists from Sun 15 Nov 2026, against simply holding Bitcoin over the same days.
