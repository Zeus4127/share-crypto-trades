How to read it. Return so far averages the arm’s positions: closed ones at the watch’s own sealed net (after its costs), open ones marked at the last close before costs (the entry session’s open to the latest close, price only). STW same days is STW over exactly those windows, and vs STW is the difference. Win rate counts closed positions only (net above zero). Early weeks are noise: a few weeks of open marks say little, and only a watch’s own declared read decides anything.
